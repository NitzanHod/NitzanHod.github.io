I am a Ph.D. candidate at the [Taub Faculty of Computer Science](https://www.cs.technion.ac.il/) at [Technion](https://www.technion.ac.il/), under the supervision of [Prof. Assaf Schuster](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=KfwgjswAAAAJ).

Currently interning at Amazon Prime Video, focusing on using generative models for improved 3D reconstruction.
